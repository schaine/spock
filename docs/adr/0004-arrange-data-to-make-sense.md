# Spock arranges data to make sense of it: Signals dashboard first, Data dashboard second

Spock doesn't just throw data at the people looking at it (Visitors and the Owner); it arranges it in ways that help them make sense of it. The Owner sees this as Spock's moat (its lasting edge over other ways of getting the same free official figures). So the main Dashboard is the Signals dashboard, which shows the Watched relationships. The Data dashboard, which shows the Indicators by Core family for exploring them one by one, comes second. We chose this over a single dashboard organised by Core family, because each Watched relationship mixes families, and grouping by family would split up the connections that give the figures their meaning.

Approved by the Owner, `schaine`, on 2026-11-19, while reviewing the starter list of Indicators (issue #3).

## Consequences

- Both Dashboards are public, as ADR 0002 says for the Dashboard.
- A new Indicator should earn its place by feeding at least one Watched relationship, or have a clear reason to stand alone on the Data dashboard.
- How the Signals dashboard groups its Watched relationships is left to the screen design. The starter-indicators research note proposes four sections that follow an AI shock through the economy.
