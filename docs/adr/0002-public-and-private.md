# Public Dashboard, private Insights, no exports

The Dashboard, Indicators, Flags, Divergences and Patterns are public: any Visitor sees them, without an account. Insights, and the place where the Owner writes them, are private to the Owner. Visitors cannot download data or connect to Spock; only the Owner's private connector can. The public part showcases the Owner's work as a digital product builder. The private Insights are kept so that, if Spock ever opens to others, the data can stay free and Insights can sit behind a paywall.

Approved by the Owner, `schaine`, on 2026-11-18, during the first grill (issue #2).

## Consequences

- No exports is a speed bump, not a wall: anything on screen can be copied, and the numbers are free from the official agencies. No heavy anti-copying measures.
- Only the Owner has an account. Visitor accounts, a paywall and a newsletter are out of scope until decided otherwise.
