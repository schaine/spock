# Spock

Spock is a macroeconomic intelligence platform that monitors key economic indicators across the US, France and the EU to identify early signs of AI-driven economic disruption, helping users anticipate risks and prepare for a new economic era.

Spock does not track how fast AI technology advances. It tracks the gap between AI progress and its economic consequences, especially for employment, income distribution and household demand.

## Language

### Geography

**Region**:
One of the economies Spock compares: the United States, France, or the European Union (all 27 member states).
_Avoid_: Country, zone, market

**European Union**:
The 27 member states taken together, not the euro area.
_Avoid_: Eurozone, euro area, Europe

### Indicators

**Indicator**:
One economic measure, published regularly for a Region, such as the unemployment rate in France.
_Avoid_: Metric, series, KPI, data point

**Indicator family**:
A group of Indicators that answer the same question about the economy. Spock has five core families and two secondary families.
_Avoid_: Category, theme, pillar

**Core family**:
One of the five Indicator families Spock focuses on, in priority order: Jobs and labour markets; Productivity, wages and income distribution; Household income and consumption; Financial conditions and business failures; AI investment and technological adoption.

**Secondary family**:
An Indicator family used only as context, because it is noisy or lags behind: Markets and prices; Public finances.

**AI-exposed occupation**:
A type of job whose tasks AI can largely perform, such as administrative work, customer support, translation or software development.
_Avoid_: At-risk job, vulnerable job

**Leading indicator**:
An Indicator that moves before the economic consequences appear, such as AI investment.

**Lagging indicator**:
An Indicator that moves after the economic consequences have happened, such as welfare spending.

### History

**Historical episode**:
A named stretch of the past that Spock compares today against: the Internet wave (1995 to 2005, the main reference), the Global financial crisis (2008 to 2010) and the Covid shock (2020 to 2021).
_Avoid_: Period, era, precedent

### What Spock tells you

**Flag**:
A notice that one Indicator is moving unusually compared with its own history, or breaks a rule the Owner wrote for it. Each Flag carries a plain factual sentence, never an interpretation.
_Avoid_: Alert, alarm, warning

**Divergence**:
Two Indicators that normally move together drifting apart, such as productivity rising while real wages stay flat.
_Avoid_: Gap, decoupling, mismatch

**Pattern**:
Several Indicators, possibly from different Indicator families or Regions, pointing the same way at the same time.
_Avoid_: Trend, cluster, signal

**Watched relationship**:
A link between Indicators that Spock checks regularly for a Divergence or a Pattern, chosen in advance with the Owner, such as productivity against wages.
_Avoid_: Correlation, rule, hypothesis

**Insight**:
The Owner's written explanation of a Flag, a Divergence or a Pattern: what is happening, why it matters and what it might signal. Insights are dated, kept forever, and private to the Owner.
_Avoid_: Analysis, commentary, interpretation, summary

### Using Spock

**Owner**:
The person who runs Spock and the only one with an account. The Owner writes Insights.

**Visitor**:
Anyone who views Spock's public pages, without an account.
_Avoid_: User, reader, subscriber, customer

**Dashboard**:
The place where a user explores Indicators and their connections over time and across Regions.
